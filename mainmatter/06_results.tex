\par The design strategy of Chapter \ref{chapter: methodology} can be assessed directly by comparing the optical distances and magnifications it prescribes with those realised on the bench. Table \ref{tab: design validation} makes that comparison for the nine configurations of the setup matrix. The in-focus distance $d_{f,th}$ and the magnification $Mag_{th}$ are the design values listed in Table \ref{tab: angled glass test matrix}, while $d_{f,exp}$ and $Mag_{exp}$ were obtained from the calibration plate placed in the in-focus plane.

\par Agreement is within 2.9\% in $d_f$ and 5.0\% in $Mag$ for every configuration except point 7. That point was the only one requiring an in-focus distance shorter than the 105 mm lens could reach, so the plane had to be set 15.6 mm further away than the design called for. The resulting 8.25\% deviation in $d_f$ propagates to 15.66\% in $Mag$, which is consistent with the thin lens relation: a relative change in $d_f$ is amplified in $Mag$ by the factor $d_f/(d_f-f)$, equal to 2.05 for this configuration.
