%Quote

The quote above always reminds me that there are more things beyond my knowledge than I can immediately see or could ever conceive. Given that this work set out to image change in transparent media, I thought it would be only proper for it to start like this. The methods that this work is built upon are a remarkable example of human ingenuity and striving to see further than the visible. Having learned something about the pioneers of schlieren imaging, I can only express admiration and respect for their work. To take mundane observations, describe them, understand them, make predictions, test them, implement the result and then improve it: this is the core of scientific discovery, and it can be seen throughout their work. With this thesis, I have tried to make a small contribution to this field based on these principles, by building on the work of those who came before me, examining it, organizing it, trying to improve it and adding something new. I hope this work helps those who will come after me and enables them to further expand our understanding of the world, inspired by the knowledge that: \emph{"There are more things in heaven and earth, [...] than are dreamt of in your philosophy"}
