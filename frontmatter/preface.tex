%Quote

The quote above always reminds me that there are more things beyond my knowledge than I can immediately see or could ever conceive. Given that this work was set out to image change in transparent media, I thought it would be only proper for it to start like this. The methods that this work is built upon are a remarkable example of human ingenuity and applied science. Having learnt some about the pioneers of schlieren imaging, I can only express admiration and respect for their work. To take mundane observations, describe them, understand them, make predictions, assess them, implement it, then improve it are the cores of scientific discovery and can be seen all over their work. With this thesis, I've tried to make a small contribution to this field based on these principles, by taking on the work of those who came before me, examining it, organizing it, trying to improve it and adding something new. I hope this work helps those who will come after me and enable them to further expand our understanding of the world, inspired by the knowledge that: \emph{"there are more things in heaven and earth, [...] than are dreamt of in your philosophy"}
