\chapter{AI Disclaimer}

AI agents have been used throughout this thesis to aid the work performed. Claude Code Opus 5 was used for writing. A first draft text was written by the author and analyzed by the AI. It then applied mechanical fixes to grammar and spelling, and signaled faults in the content, which were judged individually by the author. Opus 5 was also used to aid the coding and implementation of the post-processing methods mentioned on the text. Google's Gemini was used to improve the quality of most of the illustrative figures. Many of the pictures started as simple hand-drawing that were then submitted to Gemini, iterated and edited until the final result. The accuracy and verity of each picture was verified individually by the author. Chat GPT was used to improve the quality of the cover page image, which started as a collage of the author's own data on a word document. It was also verified by the author.
