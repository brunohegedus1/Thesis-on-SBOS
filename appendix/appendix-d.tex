\chapter{Acknowledgement of Generative AI Use}
\label{Appendix: AI Acknowledgement}

Generative AI tools were used during the preparation of this thesis, between July and September 2026. Their use is acknowledged below, following the categories set out in the TU Delft guidelines for students.

I acknowledge the use of Claude Code Opus 5 (\url{https://claude.ai/code}) to support the development of this thesis by refining language, providing feedback and assisting with programming and debugging. A first draft of each chapter was written by the author and then analyzed by the tool, which applied mechanical corrections to grammar and spelling and flagged faults in the content. Every flagged item was judged individually by the author and was accepted, modified or rejected on that basis. The tool was also used to support the coding and implementation of the post-processing methods described in this work, and to locate and summarize literature during the review, with the sources themselves consulted by the author.

I acknowledge the use of Google Gemini (\url{https://gemini.google.com}) and of ChatGPT (\url{https://chatgpt.com}) to improve the quality of the illustrative figures, a use that falls outside the categories above. Most of these figures began as simple hand-drawings by the author, which were then submitted to Gemini and iterated and edited until the final result. Where a figure is credited to another author, the tool was used only to improve the quality of the reproduced image, and the result was checked by the author against the original publication. The cover page image began as a collage of the author's own data assembled in a Word document and was refined with ChatGPT. The figures modified in this way are \ref{fig: Moving sound wave}, \ref{fig: Oblique shock geometry}, \ref{fig: jet expansion regimes}, \ref{fig: circular underexpanded jets}, \ref{fig: Diagrams of light refraction}, \ref{fig: Hooke shadowgraphy}, \ref{fig: Schlieren setup}, \ref{fig: laser speckled interferometry}, \ref{fig: BOS setup Raffel}, \ref{fig: Gojani cone of light}, \ref{fig: SBOS Meier and Roesgen}, \ref{fig: SBOS setup illustrated}, \ref{fig: Geometry BOS}, \ref{fig: SBOS with negative l}, \ref{fig: Angled glass geometry}, \ref{fig: projected speckle setup diagram}, \ref{fig: WT experiment diagram}, \ref{fig: CoC geometry} and \ref{fig: Blurred object geometry}. The accuracy and correctness of each of them were verified individually by the author against the data and the physics they represent. Every other figure in this thesis is a plot computed from measured or analytical data, a displacement or density field returned by the post-processing, a raw speckle or schlieren image, or a photograph of the experimental setup, and none of these was modified with generative AI.

As sole author of this thesis, I, Bruno Muzy Hegedüs, take sole responsibility for its content, including any errors and deviations that may have been missed by my reviews throughout the writing of this document.
